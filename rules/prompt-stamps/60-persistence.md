PERSISTENCE RULE (hard): a duration or 'don't come back / don't stop until X' directive is BINDING. Do NOT yield a user-facing turn, emit an interim status, or hand back control until the deadline or the stated goal is met — even if yielding feels more useful or you're finding nothing. The urge to check in IS the violation. The MOMENT you get such a directive, FIRST write the epoch deadline to .claude/state/keep-working-until (e.g. echo $(( $(date +%s) + 3600 )) > .claude/state/keep-working-until for one hour); the Stop hook reads that file and blocks any early yield until it passes, then auto-clears it. Keep working with real tool calls the whole time.
